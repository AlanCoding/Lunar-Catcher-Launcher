# Lunar launcher site selection

Published article: [Lunar Equator to LLO Mass Launcher Sites](https://alanse.medium.com/lunar-equator-to-llo-mass-launcher-sites-145d9351a255?sharedUserId=alanse).

[Broader LLO catcher topic](../README.md).

**[Publication packet](publication/README.md):** article order, ready-to-copy figures and tables, and the P1 lowered-release closing calculation.

## Studies and results

- **[Equatorial context](equatorial_context/README.md):** elevation versus longitude and the 144° E trajectory clearing a later mountain range. These provide the article’s introduction.
- **[Global hill search](global_hills/README.md):** 17,572 circle-clear candidates narrowed to 695 non-overlapping hubs; 42 clear eastward and 42 westward, with three in common.
- **[Four adjusted hilltops](featured_four/adjusted_hilltops/PUBLICATION.md):** final P1–P4 coordinates, dimensions and east/west flight checks. The [selection criteria](featured_four/README.md) document the preceding down-selection.
- **[Named launch ridges](launch_ridges/catalog/README.md):** 84 traced segments, scalar ridge tables, hilltop associations and the finalized 12-band equatorial map. The [expanded search](launch_ridges/expanded/README.md) contains the accepted P1–P4 terrain maps.
- **[Radial terrain profiles](radial_terrain/README.md):** maximum ground height around each radius; P1 supplies the featured graph. The [lowered-release check](radial_terrain/LOWERED_RELEASE.md) tests eastward departures down to 300 m below the summit tangent plane.

## Methods and supporting files

- [Height reference](HEIGHT_REFERENCE.md): hub-relative endpoint plane, terrain projection and curvature.
- [Shared numerical helpers](support/README.md): terrain interpolation, orbit calculations, summit screening and map rendering.
- [Terrain cache and provenance](terrain_data/README.md): cached SLDEM2015 bands and source metadata.
- [LROC QuickMap links](publication/QUICKMAP.md): P1 and P2 views supplied for selecting article photographs.

Tether sag, taper, stress and support-height tradeoffs are planned in the separate [sling-design investigation](../../sling_launcher/sag_taper_trade/README.md).
