P1 retains its 5.716 km arm radius. Shots are horizontal and eastward, with speed adjusted to reach a 25 km apoapsis. The published reference apex height is 28.68 m; subtracting it from the existing endpoint height gives −27.30 m relative to the hub tangent plane. The model uses spherical gravity, lunar rotation and the existing terrain data. Tether-shape and support sizing are a separate design step.
