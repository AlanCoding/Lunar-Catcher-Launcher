## Claims to resolve or qualify before publication

- Establish what the guide contributes relative to independent cables. Batching
  and mechanical drum coupling are features of the broader architecture.
- Distinguish calculated cable loads and energy integrals from assumed drum,
  rail and hardware sections. The heavy direct-load drum allowance remains a
  major uncertainty.
- Retain the operating-duty, material-strength, local-fabrication and lifetime
  assumptions alongside the payoff figures.
- The current shared-guide shape screens use an earlier inventory. Label them
  accordingly until they are rerun for the rigid-pair schedule.
- Keep 10 Mt/year as a separate industrial architecture investigation; its
  bidirectional catchers and continuous power change the design substantially.

The current numerical reference is available for drafting. A guide-free comparison
would sharpen the design rationale before presenting the straw as the selected
solution. If independent cables work better, the packet can become the engineering
case for a paired-drum multi-tether launcher with the straw as an explored option.
