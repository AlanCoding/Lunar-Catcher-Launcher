# Slider straw publication packets

[Design overview](../README.md) · [Publication queue](../../architecture_comparison/publication/README.md)

The conveyor-versus-tapered-tether technical note remains the first queued item.
The straw material is staged in two packets so the basic architecture can be
introduced before its engineering budget.

| Packet | Reader's question | Main material | Readiness |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [1. Concept and multi-tether prerequisite](concept/README.md) | How can gradual preparation produce a batch, and what does a shared guide add? | Original 4 → 8 → 16 → 32 power graphs/table, top-down diagrams, guide-free alternative | Draft diagram and narrative for review |
| [2. Engineering reference](engineering/README.md) | What would this machine contain, consume and deliver? | Paired drums, schedule, stresses, thermal/power budget, BOM, mass payoff | Numerical reference available; guide comparison and hardware qualification remain open |

The [guide comparison](../guide_comparison/README.md) connects the packets. It
asks whether the multi-tether launcher works better with a shared straw or with
independently moving cables. Either outcome is useful to the design.

The [industrial single-site study](../../industrial_scale/README.md) is subsequent
architecture work. Its 10 Mt/year requirements remain separate from the early-site
engineering reference.
