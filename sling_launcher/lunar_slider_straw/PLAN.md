# Remaining engineering work for the accepted design

[Reference](README.md) · [Mass model](RING_REFERENCE.md)

1. **Compare independent cables with the shared guide.** The [guide comparison](guide_comparison/README.md) retains paired drums and release throughput while removing the continuous straw. Establish cable separation, stress and energy exchange across full cycles before selecting guidance hardware.
2. **Qualify direct-load rigid drums.** Resolve wound-rope pressure, flange loads,
   shell buckling, tension redistribution, terminations, fatigue and required
   winding hardware. Replace the present heavy-section mass allowance with a
   structural design. No inter-drum gearbox is part of this architecture.
3. **Detail ground-wheel modules and six reaction tracks.** Confirm clamp preload,
   contact fatigue, bearings, suspension, dust control, structural ties and the
   stationary main rim drive. Develop local fabrication and foundation quantities.
4. **Verify 48-tether guide dynamics.** Apply the current paired schedule to a
   distributed tether/contact model, including holds, release and finite endpoint
   ramps. Confirm the straw opening, three-dimensional routing, stress and terrain
   clearance. Earlier shape results are supporting screens.
5. **Select battery and thermal hardware.** Validate the assumed 2C bidirectional
   power and shallow-cycle life, vacuum thermal protection, coolant/rotary unions,
   shaded radiator placement between ground paths, and power-electronic ratings.
6. **Integrate operations and targeting.** Lay out loading, paired holds and terminal
   batches; revise P1 trajectories for the final geometry. Add startup/shutdown,
   maintenance, replacements and missed-command handling to annual availability.

The first-year mass budget assumes existing local bulk-metal capability. Its
factory, mining fleet, catcher and LLO-to-L1 transport infrastructure require
separate budgets before a complete infrastructure payback claim.
